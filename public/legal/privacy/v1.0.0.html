Privacy Policy

Yard Quest ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our mobile application and services (the "Service").

By using Yard Quest, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you use Yard Quest, we collect information that you provide directly to us:

Account Information:
  • Name or display name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Profile photo (optional)
  • Authentication credentials (when using third-party sign-in)
Sale Listing Information:
  • Sale title and description
  • Sale address and location
  • Sale dates and times
  • Photos of items
  • Categories and featured items
Communication Information:
  • Messages sent through the app
  • Customer support inquiries
  • Feedback and reviews

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use our Service, we automatically collect certain information:

Usage Information:
  • Pages viewed and features used
  • Time spent on the app
  • Clicks, taps, and navigation patterns
  • Search queries
Device Information:
  • Device type (iPhone, Android)
  • Operating system version
  • Unique device identifiers
  • App version
  • Language preferences
Location Information:
  • Precise GPS location (with your permission)
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • Location data used for map features and nearby sales

1.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from third-party services:

  • Authentication Providers: If you sign in with Google, Apple, or another provider, we receive basic profile information (name, email, profile photo)
  • Analytics Services: We use Statsig and other analytics tools to understand how users interact with the Service

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

Purpose Examples
Provide the Service Display yard sales on map, enable messaging, process sale listings
Personalize Experience Show nearby sales, remember preferences, suggest relevant content
Communicate with You Send notifications, respond to support requests, send updates
Improve the Service Analyze usage patterns, fix bugs, develop new features
Ensure Safety Detect fraud, prevent abuse, enforce our Terms of Service
Comply with Law Respond to legal requests, protect our rights

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

3.1 With Other Users

When you list a sale or use public features:

  • Your name, profile photo, and sale details are visible to all users
  • Your exact location is shown on the map for your sale listings
  • Messages you send are visible to the recipient

3.2 With Service Providers

We share information with third-party companies that help us operate the Service:

  • Supabase: Database and authentication services
  • Mapbox: Map and location services
  • Statsig: Analytics and feature flagging
  • Cloudflare: Hosting and CDN services
  • Google/Apple: Push notifications and authentication

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and use it only for the purposes we specify.

3.3 For Legal Reasons

We may disclose your information if required by law or if we believe in good faith that such disclosure is necessary to:

  • Comply with legal obligations (court orders, subpoenas)
  • Protect our rights, property, or safety
  • Prevent fraud or security threats
  • Investigate violations of our Terms of Service

3.4 Business Transfers

If Yard Quest is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

4. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

4.1 Access and Update

You can access and update your account information anytime in the app settings.

4.2 Delete Your Account

You can delete your account at any time through the app settings. When you delete your account:

  • Your profile and personal information will be permanently deleted
  • Your sale listings will be removed
  • Your messages may remain visible to other users (but will be anonymized)
  • Some information may be retained for legal or operational purposes

4.3 Location Permissions

You can control location access through your device settings:

  • Allow location access to see nearby sales and use map features
  • Deny location access to browse without location-based features

4.4 Notifications

You can manage push notifications through:

  • App settings: Toggle notification preferences
  • Device settings: Control app notification permissions

4.5 Marketing Communications

You can opt out of promotional emails by:

  • Clicking "unsubscribe" in any marketing email
  • Adjusting preferences in app settings

Note: You cannot opt out of service-related communications (security alerts, account notifications).

4.6 Your Legal Rights (GDPR, CCPA)

Depending on your location, you may have additional rights:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal information
  • Right to Rectification: Correct inaccurate information
  • Right to Erasure: Request deletion of your information
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a portable format
  • Right to Object: Object to certain processing activities
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit how we use your information

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@yard-quest.com.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Encryption at rest for sensitive data
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and authentication
  • Secure authentication via OAuth providers

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide the Service and fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy:

  • Account Information: Until you delete your account
  • Sale Listings: Until the sale date passes or you delete the listing
  • Messages: For a reasonable period after sending
  • Usage Data: Typically 12-24 months for analytics purposes
  • Legal Acknowledgments: Permanently for compliance purposes

After deletion, we may retain certain information as required by law or for legitimate business purposes (fraud prevention, legal compliance).

7. Children's Privacy

Yard Quest is not intended for children under 13 years of age (or 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at privacy@yard-quest.com. We will take steps to delete such information.

We ensure that such transfers comply with applicable data protection laws through:

  • Standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission
  • Data processing agreements with service providers
