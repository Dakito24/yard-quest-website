1. Introduction

Yard Quest ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application ("App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

We collect several types of information from and about users of our App:

2.1 Account Information

When you create an account using Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In, we collect:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Profile photo (if provided by the authentication service)
  • Unique user identifier from the authentication provider

2.2 Profile Information

You may provide additional information to your profile, including:

  • Display name
  • Profile picture

2.3 Sale Listing Information

When you create a listing, we collect:

  • Sale or item title and description
  • Sale dates and times (for sale events)
  • Sale or item location (address and coordinates)
  • Photos of items for sale
  • Item categories, tags, price, and condition (for individual item listings)
  • Special instructions or notes

2.4 Location Information

With your permission, we collect and process location information:

  • Precise Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): GPS coordinates when you create a listing or search for nearby sales
  • Approximate Location (ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): Network-based location for general area search
  • Location Preferences: Your search area and saved locations

You can control location permissions through your device settings. Disabling location services may limit certain App features.

2.5 Camera and Photo Library

IMPORTANT (Required Disclosure): The App requests access to your device camera and photo library for the following purposes:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): To take photos of items for your sale listings
  • Photo Library: To select existing photos from your device to upload to sale listings

We do NOT:

  • Access your camera or photos without your explicit permission for each upload
  • Scan or access your photo library in the background
  • Use your photos for any purpose other than displaying your sale listings
  • Share your photos with third parties except as needed to display your public sale listings

You can disable camera and photo access in your device settings. This will prevent you from uploading photos to sale listings.

2.6 Calendar Access

IMPORTANT (Required Disclosure): The App requests access to your device calendar for the following purposes:

  • Read Calendar (android.permission.READ_CALENDAR): To check for conflicts before adding sale events
  • Write Calendar (android.permission.WRITE_CALENDAR): To add sale events to your calendar when you choose to

We do NOT:

  • Read or access your existing calendar events without your permission
  • Modify or delete existing calendar events
  • Share your calendar data with anyone
  • Use calendar data for any purpose other than adding sale events you request

Calendar access is entirely optional. You can disable it in your device settings without affecting other app features.

2.7 Communication Information

We collect information related to your communications within the App:

  • Messages sent through in-app messaging
  • Chat conversation history
  • Message timestamps

2.8 Device Information

We automatically collect certain information about your device:

  • Device type and model
  • Operating system and version
  • App version
  • Device identifiers (such as advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • Mobile network information

2.9 Usage Information

We collect information about how you use the App:

  • Features accessed and screens viewed
  • Sales viewed and favorited
  • Search queries
  • Time spent in the App
  • Interaction patterns
  • Crash reports and error logs

2.10 Push Notification Tokens

If you enable push notifications, we collect your device's push notification token to send you updates about new messages and sale updates.

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

3.1 Provide and Improve Services

  • Create and manage your account
  • Display sale listings and individual item listings on the map and in search results
  • Enable communication between users
  • Process and display your listings with photos you upload
  • Provide location-based search results and nearby sales
  • Add sale events to your calendar (when you request it)
  • Save your favorite sales
  • Improve App functionality and user experience
  • Fix technical issues and bugs

3.2 Communications

  • Send push notifications about new sales, messages, and App updates
  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests
  • Send important notices about the App or policy changes

3.3 Safety and Security

  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud and abuse
  • Enforce our Terms and Conditions
  • Protect the rights and safety of users
  • Comply with legal obligations

3.4 Analytics and Research

  • Analyze usage patterns and trends
  • Understand user preferences
  • Conduct research to improve our services
  • Generate aggregated, anonymized statistics

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

4.1 With Other Users

Certain information is visible to other users:

  • Your display name and profile photo
  • Your sale listings, including location, dates, times, and photos you upload

4.2 Service Providers

We share information with third-party service providers who perform services on our behalf:

  • Authentication: Google and Apple for sign-in services
  • Database and Hosting: Supabase for data storage and backend services
  • Analytics: Services that help us understand App usage
  • Push Notifications: Expo for delivering push notifications
  • Maps: Mapbox for displaying sale locations

These service providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and use it only for the purposes we specify.

4.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to:

  • Legal process or government requests
  • Enforcement of our Terms and Conditions
  • Protection of our rights, property, or safety
  • Protection of users or the public
  • Prevention of fraud or security issues

4.4 Business Transfers

If Yard Quest is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you of any such change.

4.5 With Your Consent

We may share your information for other purposes with your explicit consent.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. Specifically:

  • Account Information: Retained while your account is active. Upon account deletion, your profile and associated data are removed immediately, except as noted below
  • Sale Listings: Deleted immediately when you delete your account
  • Photos: Deleted from storage when you delete a sale listing or when you delete your account
  • Messages: Soft-deleted (marked as deleted) when you delete your account. Message content is no longer visible to other users
  • Calendar Events: We do not retain calendar data; events are added directly to your device calendar
  • Usage Data: Typically retained for 90 days in identifiable form, longer in aggregated form
  • Legal Acknowledgment Records: Records of your acceptance of our Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy are retained indefinitely for legal compliance and audit purposes, even after account deletion

5.1 Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time through the Account Settings screen in the App. When you delete your account:

  • Your profile information is permanently deleted
  • All your sale listings and individual item listings are permanently deleted
  • All photos you uploaded are permanently deleted from our storage
  • Your favorites are removed
  • Your conversation participation is removed and your messages are soft-deleted
  • Your push notification tokens are removed
  • Your authentication session is terminated
  • Legal acknowledgment records (timestamps and version references only) are retained for compliance

6. Your Privacy Rights

You have certain rights regarding your personal information:

6.1 Access and Correction

You can access and update most of your information through your App profile settings. For additional access or corrections, contact us through the support channels available within the App.

6.2 Deletion

You can delete your account and personal information directly through Account Settings in the App. See Section 5.1 for details on what is deleted. Note that legal acknowledgment records may be retained as required by law or for legitimate business purposes.

6.3 Data Portability

You can request a copy of your personal information in a structured, commonly used format by contacting us through the support channels available within the App.

6.4 Opt-Out Rights

  • Location Services: Disable in your device settings (Settings → Apps → Yard Quest → Permissions → Location)
  • Camera: Disable in your device settings (Settings → Apps → Yard Quest → Permissions → Camera)
  • Photo Library: Disable in your device settings (Settings → Apps → Yard Quest → Permissions → Storage/Photos)
  • Calendar: Disable in your device settings (Settings → Apps → Yard Quest → Permissions → Calendar)
  • Push Notifications: Disable in your device settings or App preferences

6.5 California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights:

  • Right to know what personal information is collected, used, shared, or sold
  • Right to delete personal information
  • Right to opt-out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information)
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise these rights, contact us through the support channels available within the App.

6.6 European Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have additional rights:

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to rectification of inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing
  • Right to withdraw consent
  • Right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority
